# What's left for HotFrog to be a daily "popcorn" game (b-20261008-124136-b89y)

_2026-10-08. Read-only gap analysis; no code changed._

## What you have

- **Core loop with plenty of feedback.** Tap to grab, a pull per grab, Perfect/Great/OK grading with popups
  (`WorldQualityPopup.client.luau`), grab recoil and idle spin, dynamic steps (Lillipad etc.), pebble
  debris (`Effects.client.luau`), lava heat build-up, a level-up arrival beat, ambience loops and per-level
  music (doc 17, deviation rows 154-176).
- **Short runs.** Death leads to a summary (score, best, NEW BEST pulse, quality counts, bugs, Flys) and a
  one-tap Replay (`MenuClient.client.luau:275-303, 383`). Normal and Classic (auto-scroll) modes.
- **Progression inside a run.** Tutorial (shown once, can be replayed), then Pot, then Kitchen at 50 steps,
  then Country at 100 (`Config.luau:169`). Pot has height stages, Kitchen has an intro ramp, and the Pot
  rim works as a landmark.
- **Meta.** Flys come from bugs (5 each, golden bugs 25: `Config.luau:386,414`). There are 9 skins:
  4 bought with Flys for 500-2000, 4 Game Pass skins, and the default (`SkinCatalog.luau`). The store
  shows a full-screen frog preview.
- **Return hook.** A free gift on an escalating cooldown. The wait is `exp(seed)` clamped to 2 min-6 h,
  and the seed only ever goes up, so after about 10 claims it is a flat 6 h
  (`GiftService.server.luau:15-16,69,94`).
- **Social.** One lane per player. A race rail shows every player's height plus the level landmarks
  (`GameClient.client.luau:966-1110`). There is an all-time global top-10 from an `OrderedDataStore`
  (`Profiles.luau:51,313`) and a Top Scores panel.
- **Roblox plumbing.** DataStore profiles, purchase receipts, badge code (10-100 steps), Game Pass and
  Developer Product handling, touch, mouse and gamepad input.

## What's missing, highest impact per effort first

1. **Ship blockers (only you can do these; S each).** The real ids are still zero: badges
   (`Badges.luau`, all `id = 0`), Game Passes (`SkinCatalog.luau:21-24`) and Fly products
   (`SkinService.server.luau:20`, an empty table). So right now badges, premium skins and Fly packs
   silently do nothing. Most of the ~107 bug-loop fixes say "not playtested". A 30-minute phone playtest
   (portrait 9:16) through Pot, Kitchen and Country, plus one 2-player session, is the biggest single
   unknown. You also need an icon, 3+ thumbnails, and a short description for discovery. None of these
   are tracked in the repo. Files: the three above, plus the Creator Hub.

2. **Daily / weekly leaderboard (S-M).** The only board is all-time (`HotfrogGlobalBest_v1`). Within a
   week it fills with scores new players can never reach, so it stops motivating anyone. A day-keyed
   `OrderedDataStore` (for example `Daily_<yyyymmdd>`), and optionally a weekly one, with tabs in Top
   Scores, gives a board that resets and a reason to come back each day. Files: `Profiles.luau`
   (`submitBest`/`topScores`), `GameServer.server.luau:2048` (GetTopScores), `MenuClient.client.luau:172+`.

3. **Daily login streak / daily reward (M).** The gift is a cooldown, not a daily habit. It doesn't
   count consecutive days or reward coming back tomorrow, and nothing tells the player it is ready.
   A day-N streak (for example 50 → 75 → 100 … Flys, with day 7 giving a golden-bug swarm or a skin
   shard), shown on the main menu, is the standard D1/D7 lever. Files: `Profiles.luau` (`lastLoginDay`,
   `streak`), `GiftService.server.luau` or a new `DailyService.server.luau`, `MenuClient.client.luau`.

4. **Combo / Perfect-streak juice (S-M).** Grades are tracked but each grab stands alone. A visible
   Perfect-chain counter (escalating pitch, small screen punch, `HapticService` pulse on mobile, a
   bigger burst at x5 or x10) makes every 60-second run feel skilful and gives players a second thing
   to chase besides height. There is no camera shake or haptics anywhere in `src/client`. Files:
   `WorldQualityPopup.client.luau`, `GameClient.client.luau` (camera), `SfxEvents.client.luau`, and
   `Config.luau` for tuning. Whether it also scores points is an open choice (below).

5. **Long-tail badges (S).** All 10 badges are for 10-100 steps in one run, so a decent player earns
   every one in the first session. Add badges for reaching Kitchen, reaching Country, 250 steps,
   100 / 1000 bugs caught in total, first golden bug, owning every Fly skin, a 7-day streak and
   50 Perfects in a run. Badges show on the game page and pull players back. Files: `Badges.luau`,
   `BadgeService.server.luau`, and lifetime counters in `Profiles.luau`.

6. **Something to spend Flys on after day 3 (M).** The Fly skins cost 5000 Flys in total. At about
   5 Flys per bug that is a few days of play, and then Flys are worth nothing. Options: more Fly skins,
   cheap cosmetics (tongue colour, hand trail, death splash), or a one-time "continue" per run.
   Content plus `SkinCatalog.luau`, `SkinService.server.luau`, `StoreUI.client.luau`. See the open
   choices.

7. **Daily missions (M).** 3 small goals a day ("catch 10 bugs", "5 Perfects in a row",
   "reach Kitchen") paying Flys. Each one gives a reason for a session today and short-term goals that
   don't depend on skill. Files: new server service, `Profiles.luau`, a menu panel, plus hooks on
   the existing `BugCaughtServer` / `ScoreChanged` signals.

8. **Social pull (S-M).** Mark "your best" and your friends' bests on the race rail (a ghost line).
   Show "beat X's score" on the summary. Add an invite button (`SocialService:PromptGameInvite`).
   Today other lanes are off-screen (`LANE_SPACING` 46 against a portrait half-width of about 8.8), so
   you only see other players as rail markers. Files: `GameClient.client.luau` (rail), `Profiles.luau`
   (friends' bests need a lookup by UserId), `MenuClient.client.luau`.

9. **"Gift ready" re-engagement (M).** Roblox Experience Notifications, sent from Open Cloud, for
   "your gift / streak is ready". This needs an opt-in prompt in game and a server-side sender. Files:
   `GiftService.server.luau`, `tools/`.

10. **Content past Country (L).** After 100 steps the run is endless Country. Fine for popcorn runs,
    but a 4th level (or rotating "daily biome" variants of the existing three) keeps a 100+ player
    curious. Files: `Levels.luau`, `StepKinds.luau`, `SceneryKinds.luau`, plus new art and music.

11. **Known polish gaps (doc 17 "Known gaps", S-M each).** Non-centre sprite pivots still render
    centred (Grass, Castle, Rocket, Flame…). The `Flingee` splash is a hard-coded 1.2-stud part.
    `Chef` and `US` scenery overhang the next lane. Classic scroll speeds were never tuned by playing.
    Step spawn width on landscape screens (`SPAWN_VIEW_HALF_WIDTH`) is unchecked.

## Open choices (yours, not decided here)

- **Leaderboards:** daily only, or daily + weekly + all-time? Also, should Classic get its own board?
  This is already an open gap in doc 17 ("One best score for both run modes").
- **Continue / revive:** offer it at all? For Flys only, or also Robux (a Developer Product)? Paid
  continues earn money but make leaderboards unfair.
- **Combo scoring:** does a Perfect streak add score (it changes leaderboards and the 1-point-per-step
  rule from Unity), or is it only feedback plus a missions stat?
- **Gift escalation:** keep Unity's permanent 6 h cooldown, reset it daily, or replace it with the
  streak?
- **Server size:** lanes don't interact except through the rail. Choose small servers (6-8, a lively
  rail) or 1-2 (solo feel). This is set in the Creator Hub, not in the repo.
- **Daily seeded climb:** the same step layout for everyone each day (Wordle-style comparison). It's
  attractive, but `Lane.luau` would need a per-day seeded RNG, and it overlaps with item 2.

## Suggested order

Do 1 (yours) first, then 2, 3 and 4 as one "daily loop" pass. Do 5 alongside badge creation in 1.
After that, 6 and 7 once the playtest shows how fast players earn Flys.

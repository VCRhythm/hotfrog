# Fly sinks for players who own every skin (b-20261008-141652-rdl8)

_2026-10-08. Brainstorm, read-only; no code changed. Follows gap 6 of b-20261008-124136-b89y._

## The economy today (real numbers)

**What there is to buy.** Four Fly skins: Blue 500, Hawt 1000, Space 1500, Mystery 2000, so
**5000 Flys** for the whole set (`src/shared/SkinCatalog.luau`). The other four are Game Pass skins.
After that, `BuyWithFlys` is the only thing that spends Flys (`SkinService.server.luau:43-54`).
There is no unused frog art: every folder under `Sprites/Frogs/` is already in the catalog.

**Bugs, the main faucet** (`Config.luau:380-419`, `BugService.server.luau:360-372, 503-537`):
- Each lane spawns on a 2 s tick, **with no RunState gate**, so it runs on the Menu and Dead screens
  too (doc 17 attract mode). At most 8 bugs are on screen at once.
- A tick gives a single bug 88% of the time. A single is worth 5 Flys, or 25 if golden (6%), so
  6.2 on average. The other 12% of ticks give a swarm of 3-5 bugs at 5 each, about 20 Flys.
- That averages about **7.9 Flys per tick**, so a player who catches everything gets about
  **235 Flys/min**.
- During a run the hands are busy grabbing. A guessed 25-40% catch rate gives **about 60-95 Flys/min**,
  or roughly **50-100 Flys per 60 s popcorn run**.

**Gift** (`GiftService.server.luau:13-16, 69, 94`): 100 Flys per claim. The wait after each claim is
2 min (claims 1-5), then 2.5 min, 6.7 min, 18 min, 50 min, 2.25 h, and a flat 6 h from the 11th claim
on. A first session collects about 800 Flys in roughly 35 minutes. After that the gift is worth at
most 400 Flys a day, and realistically 100-200.

**Robux.** `FLY_PRODUCTS` is empty. The planned pack is "1000 Flys" (`SkinService.server.luau:19-22`).

**What this means.** The whole skin set takes about **1-1.5 hours of play**: roughly 50-70 runs plus
the early gifts, which is two or three sessions. After that a regular player banks about
**75 Flys per run** with nothing to spend it on, and the 1000-Fly Robux pack has no reason to exist.

**Pricing targets used below:**
- Something bought again and again should cost **1-3 runs of income (75-250 Flys)**.
- One-off cosmetics should sit near **existing skin prices (300-2000)**.
- There should also be **one sink with no ceiling** for hoarders.

**Caveat for every price.** Catching bugs on the menu, without playing, can approach 235 Flys/min.
So any sink priced off run income is cheap for someone who taps flies on the menu. Either accept that
(it is still play), or cap bug spawns while not in a run. Doc 17 calls menu bugs deliberate, so that
cap is your call.

## Ideas, ranked

### 1. Fly Jar: prestige titles (S-M). Recommended
- **What:** a "Fly Jar" panel where you feed Flys into a jar that never gives them back.
- **Tiers:** Jar tiers at 1k / 2.5k / 5k / 10k / 25k / 50k / 100k total fed. Each tier unlocks a
  title ("Fly Snacker" … "Lord of the Flies").
- **Where titles show:** next to your marker on the race rail, and on your Top Scores row.
- **Badges:** one per tier (pairs with the long-tail badge idea, gap 5).
- **Price:** no ceiling. Feed in 100 / 500 / all.
- **Why it fits:** the race rail is the only place players see each other (lanes are 46 studs apart,
  off-screen). A title on the rail is the cheapest way to show off there. It has **no gameplay or
  leaderboard effect**, so it needs no balancing, and it gives the 1000-Fly pack a reason to exist.
- **Files:**
  - `Profiles.luau`: a `jarFlys` field, plus merge and save.
  - `SkinService.server.luau`: a `FeedJar` RemoteFunction through the same checked-spend path as
    `BuyWithFlys`. It also writes a `Title` attribute on the player.
  - `GameClient.client.luau`: the rail label (around lines 966-1110).
  - `MenuClient.client.luau`: the jar panel and the title on the Top Scores row.
  - `Badges.luau`: tier badges, optional.
  - That is 4-5 files with no new art. A jar sprite is optional.

### 2. Skin colourways (M). Recommended
- **What:** each owned skin gets 3-4 tints (for example Ember, Mint, Midnight, Gold) that you buy once
  and pick in the store.
- **Price:** 400 / 600 / 800, and Gold 1500. That is about 3300 per skin, or **about 16-25k Flys**
  across the Fly and Game Pass skins, 3-5x the skin set.
- **Why it fits:** the frog is the only thing the player looks at, and every frog part is already a
  textured Decal. It costs no new art, and the full-screen store preview already exists to show it off.
- **Technical catch:** `WorldFrogCosmetics.client.luau:268` tweens the decal `Color3` to the heat tint
  and back to **white**. The cooled-down colour has to become the colourway colour, and the heat tint
  should be multiplied over it rather than replace it. `Color3` multiplies, so it can only darken.
  Pick the tints on the lightest art (check Hot Frog first), or limit them to body and limbs and leave
  the eyes alone.
- **Files:**
  - `SkinCatalog.luau`: a shared `Tints` table.
  - `Profiles.luau`: `ownedTints` and `selectedTint`.
  - `SkinService.server.luau`: `BuyTint` / `SelectTint`, with the tint replicated as a frog attribute.
  - `WorldFrogCosmetics.client.luau`: the base colour.
  - `StoreUI.client.luau`: a swatch row on the preview.
  - About 5 files.

### 3. Second Chance continue (M). Recommended, but needs your ruling
- **What:** on death, the Dead screen shows a "Continue" button for a few seconds. It respawns you on
  your last held step with your score kept.
- **Price:** **150 Flys** for the first continue in a run (about 2 runs' income), **300** for the
  second, and at most 2 per run.
- **Why it fits:** it is the classic popcorn-game sink, it comes back every run, and it kicks in
  exactly when a good run is about to end, which is when players most want to spend.
- **Open choice:** this is already listed in the earlier report's "Continue / revive" choice. Flys can
  be bought with Robux, so continues are pay-to-win on the boards. A safe default:
  - continued runs still count toward your personal best;
  - they are **not** submitted to the global or daily board, **or** they show a small marker there;
  - Classic mode gets none.
- **Files:**
  - `GameServer.server.luau`: `killFrog` (around line 1339) holds a "pending continue" state before
    going to `Dead`. Resume reuses the respawn code (around 1399/1967) at the last grabbed step's
    position.
  - A `ContinueRun` remote in the remote list.
  - `MenuClient.client.luau`: the button and countdown on the death summary.
  - `Config.luau`: prices.
  - About 3-4 files. The risk is in GameServer's run-state flow.

### 4. Catch and grab effects (M-L)
- **What:** cosmetic slots that don't change gameplay:
  - tongue colour: 250;
  - catch-burst colour or confetti: 400;
  - grab-impact puff (recolour the pebble debris in `Effects.client.luau`): 400;
  - death splash variant (the `Flingee` splash, gold or rainbow): 800-1500.
- **Total:** 2-4k per set, more as slots grow.
- **Why it fits:** these are the moments that already repeat every second (grab, catch, death), so a
  bought effect is seen constantly.
- **Files:** a new `CosmeticCatalog.luau`, plus `Profiles.luau`, `SkinService.server.luau`,
  `Effects.client.luau`, `WorldBugs.client.luau` (tongue), and a new tab in `StoreUI.client.luau`.
  About 6 files plus some particle and art work.

### 5. Practice tickets: start at Kitchen or Country (M)
- **What:** pay to start a run at Kitchen (**100 Flys**) or Country (**200 Flys**). The score does not
  count for best or the boards.
- **Why it fits:** skilled players die late and have to replay Pot every time to practise the hard
  levels. This sells them time, not power.
- **Files:** `Lane.luau` (starting level index), `GameServer.server.luau` (a `StartRun` payload field,
  and skipping `submitBest`), `MenuClient.client.luau` (mode picker), `Config.luau`.
- **Risk:** the Tutorial → Pot ladder and the Kitchen intro-ramp transitions assume a run starts at the
  bottom.

### 6. Bug Bomb: a social prank (S)
- **What:** spend **100 Flys** to drop a swarm of 15 harmless, non-paying flies into another player's
  lane, labelled with your name ("from Tristan!").
- **Why it fits:** today the only interaction between lanes is the rail, and this is a cheap,
  friendly one.
- **Files:** `SpawnGiftFlys` already bursts unrewarded flies into a lane
  (`GiftService.server.luau:55-63`, `BugService` fan-in around line 87). So this is a remote in
  `SkinService` or `BugService`, a target picker in `MenuClient` or on the rail, and a cooldown so it
  can't be used to grief someone's run. About 3 files.
- **Risk:** it could be distracting in the middle of a run. Limit it to targets who are on the Menu or
  Dead screen, or spawn the swarm off to one side.

### 7. Run boosters (M). Not recommended yet
Examples are Bug Magnet (2x golden chance for one run, 100 Flys) and Lava Shield (one heat reset,
200 Flys).
- Bug Magnet tends to **make** more Flys than it costs, which is the opposite of a sink.
- Lava Shield changes scores, so it has the same leaderboard problem as continue without being as
  satisfying.

### 8. Mystery egg or random cosmetic roll. Avoid
Flys can be bought with Robux, so a random item for Flys counts as a paid random item under Roblox
policy (odds disclosure, regional restrictions). Use direct purchases only.

### 9. More Fly skins (L)
More skins are the most natural sink, but there is no spare frog art (every `Sprites/Frogs/` folder is
already used), so each skin means drawing a full rig. Keep the "Gold" colourway (idea 2) as the cheap
stand-in.

## Recommendation

Build in this order:
1. **Fly Jar** (S-M, no ceiling, no balance risk).
2. **Skin colourways** (M, about 16-25k Flys of content with no new art).
3. **Second Chance** (M, 150/300 per run), once you decide how continued runs treat the leaderboards.

Together they cover the three kinds of sink: one with no ceiling, one-off collection, and one that
comes back every run. Set final prices after the first playtest measures real Flys per run. The
50-100 per run above is an estimate from spawn rates, not a measurement.
